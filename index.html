About Vivek

Art Created from
the Heart

Vivek is a Hyderabad-based fine artist known for hyper-realistic pencil portraits, glass engraving, and vibrant spiritual paintings. Every piece is made with museum-quality materials and obsessive attention to detail.

From wedding gifts to memorial portraits to pooja-room centrepieces, Vivek Arts creates pieces that families treasure for generations.
